01. About Me

I'm a full-stack software engineer with deep experience shipping production-grade applications that integrate AI, real-time communication, multilingual voice pipelines, and hardened backend infrastructure. My work spans the entire stack — from database schema design and async background workers to polished, reactive frontends.
